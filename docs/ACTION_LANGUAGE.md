# callx402 Action Language

The following phrases are **behavioral usage language**: short, plain ways to
say "use callx402 to do this thing." They describe what the action layer is
for. They are **not** trademark claims, not exclusivity claims, and not an
assertion of ownership over any phrase.

- "Need to diagnose x402? callx402."
- "Need to rescue a transaction? callx402."
- "Need to route a paid agent job? callx402."
- "Need settlement certainty? callx402."
- "Need to execute under a budget? callx402."

Usage notes:

- Use them to point someone at the action layer when the job matches:
  diagnosis, rescue, routing, settlement resolution, or budgeted execution.
- Do not present them as slogans implying callx402 is the only way to do
  these things, or that the phrase itself is a protected mark. It is not.
- Do not attach them to the flagship product brand (which is separate and
  forthcoming). They describe the action, not the product.
- In formal or legal contexts, prefer the plain description: "callx402 is the
  universal action layer that dispatches into existing x402 subsystems."
